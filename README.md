# Hi, I'm Jeremy 👋

**AI Evaluation Specialist · Prompt Engineer · Music Producer**

I work where AI meets audio. I create and review training data that teaches AI systems about sound and music, and I've been writing, recording, and producing my own music for years.

## What I do

- **AI evaluation and training data:** I write audio tasks and the rubrics used to grade them, from mixing goals and loudness targets (LUFS, peak ceilings) to clear, observable grading criteria.
- **Quality review:** I review other contributors' tasks and rubrics, catch gaps, and give written feedback that helps them meet the standard.
- **Image description accuracy:** I've labeled and refined AI-generated image descriptions, checking that every object, position, and detail is really in the image.

## Why this work matters

A lot of people are still uneasy about AI, and I understand why. These systems are becoming part of how we search, create, learn and make decisions, and they learn from the examples people give them. That's where my work sits. Every task I write, every rubric I review and every AI-generated description I check is a chance to catch something wrong before it becomes part of what a model learns: a detail that isn't really in an image, a confident answer that isn't true, or a standard too vague to hold anyone to. I take that responsibility seriously. To me, good evaluation means holding AI to the same values I'd hold a person to: be honest about what you know, get the facts right, and keep growing when you fall short. Human judgment is still one of the most important safeguards in this field, and I want to be one of the people making sure this technology grows in a direction people can trust. We are here to enforce safety, truth, and integrity within the AI/SI space.
